Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

TL;DR: Your drinks, goals and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. From version 1.5.1 the app sends anonymous usage statistics (never a drink amount or anything you enter) to help us improve it, and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy.

1. Data We Collect

Vessel has no user accounts and no login, and it never collects personal data. The only thing that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described in section 5, which you can turn off.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter into Vessel is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Logged drinks (type, amount, timestamp, optional notes)
  • Settings (daily goal, preferred unit, reminder schedule, theme, body weight)
  • Quick-add presets

This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Vessel offers optional integration with Apple Health. If you enable this in Settings:

  • When you log a drink, water and caffeine amounts are written to HealthKit on your device
  • Apple Health data stays on your device and is encrypted by iOS
  • You can revoke permission any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health

Vessel never reads HealthKit data for any purpose other than displaying your own intake within the app. Health data is never included in the usage statistics below.

4. Notifications

If you enable hydration reminders, Vessel uses Apple's local notification system to send you reminders on your chosen schedule. Notifications are scheduled on your device and nothing about them is sent to us.

5. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To learn which features help people and where the app goes wrong, Vessel sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (hosted by Supabase). This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data; turning it off stops collection and deletes anything not yet sent.

What is sent: a random installation ID created for this app (it is deleted with the app); device model, iOS and app version; language, region and time zone; accessibility settings (such as larger text or reduced motion); when sessions start and end and the last screen viewed; the app's own events (for example that a drink was logged and its drink type, that a drink was edited or deleted, that the daily goal was reached or that onboarding was finished — never the amount, time, note or goal); paywall views and taps; subscription start, trial, renewal status, cancellation and refund events; whether the app asked for a review; and Apple's anonymous crash and performance reports (MetricKit).

What is never sent: your name, email, phone or address; your location; the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendors (IDFV); prices you paid, receipts or transaction IDs; Apple Health data; and anything you enter or create — no drink amounts, notes, goals, body weight or reminder times. Your IP address is not stored or used. Statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used for tracking or advertising.

Retention: usage statistics are kept for 13 months, crash and performance reports for 6 months. Deletion: email us the Installation ID shown in Settings › Privacy and we will delete everything recorded for it.

Other than that, the app makes no network requests except for Apple StoreKit purchase and subscription entitlement checks. Vessel contains no advertising, no social logins and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Vessel offers an optional Vessel Pro subscription. Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit:

  • Payment is handled by Apple; we never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.)
  • We receive only anonymized transaction records from Apple to verify your subscription entitlement
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

7. Children's Privacy

Vessel is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal data from anyone; the anonymous usage statistics in section 5 are not linked to any person.

8. Your Rights

Your drink data lives only on your device, so all control over it is yours:

  • To delete all drink data: Settings → Data → "Delete all drink data"
  • To revoke HealthKit access: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Vessel
  • To stop usage statistics: Settings → Privacy → turn off "Share Usage Data"
  • To delete usage statistics already sent: email us the Installation ID from Settings → Privacy
  • To delete everything on your device: Delete the app from your iPhone

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we'll update this policy and note the change at the top.
